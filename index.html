AI-Powered Hiring

MiraTech AI evaluates candidates instantly

Paste a job description, upload resumes, and let MiraTech AI score every candidate in seconds. No bias, no guesswork — just objective match scores.

1. Post a job description
Add your role requirements — skills, experience, responsibilities. The more detail, the sharper the AI match.
2. Browse candidates and resumes
Drop one or multiple PDFs. Resumes are read in memory — never stored — and each candidate gets their own score card.
3. MiraTech AI scores each resume
The AI reads the resume against your job description and generates a JRC score — Job Description × Resume Comparison — from 0 to 100.
4. Get an objective ranking
Candidates are ranked by Match Score = (UPC + JRC) ÷ 2. Export the report, unlock contacts, and hire faster.
AI Resume Matching is available from Starter tier onwards.

Inclusive Education · AI for Schools

Fair Exams for Every Student

MEEB — MiraTech Exam Evaluation Bot — is an AI designed to evaluate student exams without bias or preconceptions. It looks at how students think, create and solve problems — not just their final grade.

In Development · Join Early
Free for community schools at launch
Evaluation Dimensions
Beyond the Final Grade

MEEB evaluates five interconnected dimensions that reveal what a student truly knows and can do — no fixed score, no prejudice.

📖
Content Mastery
How well the student understood and applied the subject matter — depth, accuracy and contextual use.
🛠️
Exercise Development
The quality of the reasoning path, clarity of steps and methodological consistency across each exercise.
🧩
Structured Thinking
The student's logical flow, argument organisation and ability to connect ideas coherently from problem to conclusion.
💡
Creative Methods
Original approaches, alternative solutions and innovative thinking that go beyond the expected — rewarding intellectual courage.
🎯
Final Outcome
The result in context — weighted against effort, growth and the difficulty of the path taken to reach it.
How It Works
Three Steps to Honest Evaluation
01
Teacher uploads the exam
The educator sends the exam sheet and the student's answer — typed text, handwritten scan or structured file. MEEB receives it with zero student history.
02
MEEB reads with fresh eyes
The AI analyses the answer across all five dimensions without knowing who the student is, their previous grades or what the teacher expects.
03
Honest, detailed feedback
The teacher receives a rich report — per-dimension analysis, written justifications, highlighted strengths and personalised development suggestions for each student.
Real-World Advantages
What Changes When MEEB Evaluates
Zero preconceptionThe AI has no memory of the student's past — every exam is a clean slate evaluated purely on its own merits.
Creativity is rewardedStudents who solve problems in unexpected ways get recognised — not penalised for being different.
Teachers save timeMEEB drafts the detailed evaluation; teachers review, adjust and sign off — cutting correction time significantly.
Actionable feedbackEvery report includes specific, written guidance — not just a number — so students know exactly how to improve.
Equity across schoolsCommunity schools access the same quality of evaluation as elite institutions — levelling the educational playing field.
Data-driven insightSchools build anonymised aggregate reports on class-wide strengths and gaps — powering smarter curriculum decisions.
